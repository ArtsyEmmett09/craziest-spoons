Diamond Dove Facts

This information was obtained from the Wikipedia page on diamond doves (the link is just below this list!), so it may not be completely accurate.

  • The scientific name for diamond doves is Geopelia cuneata.
  • They are quite small, only 9 to 11 in. (19 to 21 cm.) in length.
  • The "diamond" in their name references the specks on their wings.
  • They prefer walking over flying, because they find their food (ants and grass seeds) on the ground.
  • Their calls are mournful, soft, and pleasant. The sequence of diamond dove calls is: "coooo, coooo... coooo, coo, coooo."

Caring for a Diamond Dove

Diamond Doves make great pets for bird lovers, but you need to know how to care for one! Here's some information from the Spruce Pets!

  • Diamond doves have an average lifespan of 10 years.
  • They are gentle, but also fragile. Handle with care! But still make sure to stick around, as they are very social!
  • You may want to get two, so that they don't get lonely. They might not want to interact with you that much.
  • Because of their preference for walking, a more horizontal cage is better for a diamond dove than a vertical one.
  • Diamond doves are prone to red mites, as well as respiratory problems like canker and pneumonia.
  • The bulk of a diamond dove's diet should be seed, but they'll need some grit added because of their beaks. The occasional fresh greens, soft fruits, and a little hard-boiled egg is also good for your dove.
  • Finally, create a safe flight zone for your bird so they can get some exercise! You could put some mesh wire with a ceiling in an area of your yard so that your dove can fly without fear. Clipped wings are never humane!

Diamond Dove Classification

The taxonomic classification of diamond doves is as follows:

  • Domain: Eukarya
  • Kingdom: Animalia
  • Phylum: Chordata
  • Class: Aves
  • Order: Columbiformes
  • Family: Columbidae
  • Genus: Geopelia
  • Species: Cuneata

In simpler terms, diamond doves have a nucleus in their cells, they are animals, they have a backbone, they are birds, they are, of course, doves, and they are their own type of dove.
